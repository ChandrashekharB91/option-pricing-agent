Method and assumptions

Each trading day at the close, the nearest weekly NIFTY expiry (0.9 to 8 days away) is rebuilt from NSE's bhavcopy: parity forward, implied vols and the SVI smile. Entry is at the close plus or minus 0.25% (at least one tick); strikes with no volume that day are not tradeable. Charges use today's schedule for every day (config/charges.json (current)); flat 6.5% rate. Each strategy is settled at the index close on expiry day. Ideas are per strategy unit (1 lot); capital sizing is not applied. The model view uses only closes before the entry day.
